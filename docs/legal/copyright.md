# Copyright & Takedown Policy | គោលការណ៍កម្មសិទ្ធិបញ្ញា និងការដកចេញ

_Last updated / ធ្វើបច្ចុប្បន្នភាពចុងក្រោយ: 2026-10-01_

SokSan respects the intellectual property of others and expects its
community to do the same. | សកសានគោរពកម្មសិទ្ធិបញ្ញារបស់អ្នកដទៃ។

## Reporting infringement | ការរាយការណ៍ការរំលោភ

Use the in-app **Report** action (⋯ on any post) and choose *Copyright
violation / រំលោភកម្មសិទ្ធិបញ្ញា*, or email copyright@soksan.app with:

1. The infringing content's URL or post ID.
2. A description of the original work and where it is published.
3. Your contact details and a good-faith statement that you are the
   rights holder (or act on their behalf).
4. Your physical or electronic signature.

## Our response | ការឆ្លើយតបរបស់យើង

- Valid notices: content is removed or access disabled, usually within
  48 hours; the uploader is notified without your personal details being
  shared beyond what the law requires.
- Repeat infringers lose their accounts.
- Counter-notices: if you believe your content was removed in error,
  reply to the notification email with your counter-notice; we restore
  content within 14 days unless the reporter pursues legal action.

## Abuse of this process | ការប្រើប្រាស់ខុស

Knowingly filing false copyright claims to remove legitimate content is
itself a Community Guidelines violation and may lead to account
restriction. | ការដាក់ពាក្យបណ្តឹងក្លែងក្លាយដោយចេតនា គឺជាការរំលោភ
