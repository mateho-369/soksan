# Privacy Policy | គោលការណ៍ឯកជនភាព

_Last updated / ធ្វើបច្ចុប្បន្នភាពចុងក្រោយ: 2026-10-01_

SokSan ("we") helps people discover Cambodia's hidden gems. This policy
explains what we collect, what we refuse to collect, and the controls you
have. ខ្មែរ: សូមអានផ្នែកខាងក្រោមជាភាសាខ្មែរ។

## What we collect | ទិន្នន័យដែលយើងប្រមូល

- **Account**: name, email, password (hashed), optional profile details.
- **Your posts**: photos, captions, and the location you choose.
- **Interactions**: likes, comments, bookmarks, follows, reports — needed
  to run the community and keep it safe.

## Location privacy — the important part | ឯកជនភាពទីតាំង

Every post with a pin offers three precision choices, and the default is
**approximate**:

1. **Exact (ប្រកដ្ឋានច្បាស់លាស់)** — your precise point. Always visible to you
   and moderators; shown to others only if you explicitly chose it.
2. **Approximate (ប្រហែល) — default** — coordinates are rounded publicly to
   ~110 m; the map shows the area, not the exact spot.
3. **Sensitive (រសើប)** — rounded to ~1.1 km and flagged; use this for
   fragile ecosystems, private homes or anywhere attention could cause harm.

Nearby search uses your queried point only to rank public posts; your own
location is never stored or shared. You can edit or delete a post's
location choice at any time.

## External services (complete list) | សេវាកម្មខាងក្រៅ (បញ្ជីពេញលេញ)

SokSan talks to exactly two external origins, both required for the app
to render — none is tracking:

1. **OpenFreeMap** (`tiles.openfreemap.org`) — public map tiles built
   from OpenStreetMap. Requests carry no account identity; no API key,
   no cookies, no registration. Attribution is shown on the map.
2. **Cloudflare R2** — storage for media you upload (production only).

**Fonts are self-hosted** (Inter, Kantumruy Pro, Bricolage Grotesque,
and the Khmer typeface Hanuman ship from our own origin as woff2). There
are no requests to Google, no font CDNs, and no other third-party origins.

សកសានប្រើសេវាកម្មខាងក្រៅតែ ២ ប៉ុណ្ណោះ — ផែនទីបើកចំហ
(OpenFreeMap) និងកន្លែងរក្សាទុកមេឌា (Cloudflare R2)។ ពុម្ពអក្សរ
(រួមទាំងអក្សរខ្មែរ Hanuman) រក្សាទុកក្នុងម៉ាស៊ីនរបស់យើងផ្ទាល់ —
គ្មានការស្នើសុំទៅកាន់ភាគីទីបីណាមួយឡើយ។

## What we refuse | អ្វីដែលយើងមិនធ្វើ

- **No tracking, no analytics, no advertising networks.** No cookies used
  for tracking; no fingerprinting; no data sold or shared with brokers.
- **No third-party push notifications** — notifications are in-app only.
- Map tiles come from **OpenFreeMap** (open data); tile requests do not
  carry your account identity. We do not use Google Maps for the public map.

## Sharing | ការចែករំលែក

We share data only: with your consent; with service providers under
contract (hosting, storage, email delivery) who process it for us; or when
lawfully required. Reports you file are shown to moderators without your
identity being revealed to the reported author.

## Retention & deletion | ការរក្សាទុក និងការលុប

- Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete (password required).
  Your identity is anonymized immediately and all sessions revoked; posts
  and reports are retained in anonymized form for moderation integrity,
  then purged after 12 months.
- We keep logs up to 14 days, backups up to 12 months.

## Your rights | សិទ្ធិរបស់អ្នក

Access, correct, export or delete your data by emailing
privacy@soksan.app (or in-app). We answer within 30 days.

## Changes | ការផ្លាស់ប្តូរ

Material changes are announced in-app at least 14 days ahead.
