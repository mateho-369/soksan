# Report Abuse & Safety | ការរាយការណ៍ និងសុវត្ថិភាព

_Last updated / ធ្វើបច្ចុប្បន្នភាពចុងក្រោយ: 2026-10-01_

## How to report | របៀបរាយការណ៍

- **Content**: tap ⋯ on any post → *Report post / រាយការណ៍ប្រកាស*, pick a
  reason and add optional details. Comments have the same flow.
- **A person**: from the same dialog you can *Block / ទប់ស្កាត់* (stops all
  interaction both ways) or *Mute / បិទសំឡេង* (hides their posts from your
  feed only).
- **Urgent safety issues** (imminent harm, exploitation of minors):
  email safety@soksan.app — we review these within 4 hours.

## What happens next | អ្វីដែលកើតឡើងបន្ទាប់

1. Your report is logged with a closed reason list; your identity is
   never revealed to the reported author.
2. If several reports accumulate, the content is hidden automatically
   while it waits for review (this is reversible and not a verdict).
3. A moderator decides: uphold (content removed, author notified), or
   dismiss (content stays). All decisions are audit-logged.
4. Appeals: reply to the in-app notification or email
   safety@soksan.app; a second moderator reviews.

## Reporter protections | ការការពារអ្នករាយការណ៍

- Filing a report in good faith never harms your account.
- Weaponizing reports, blocks or mutes to harass someone is a violation.
- We never share reporter identities with reported users.
