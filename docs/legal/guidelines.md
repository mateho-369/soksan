# Community Guidelines | គោលការណ៍សហគមន៍

_Last updated / ធ្វើបច្ចុប្បន្នភាពចុងក្រោយ: 2026-10-01_

SokSan exists to share Cambodia responsibly. These rules apply to posts,
comments, profiles, collections and messages.

## Be a good guest | ធ្វើជាភ្ញៀវល្អ

- Respect people and places. No harassment, hate speech, threats or
  bullying (មិនមានការរំខាន ការស្អប់ខ្ពើម ការគំរាមកំហែង ឬការប្រមាថ)។
- Share honestly. No fake reviews, fake places, or misleading photos.

## Protect hidden gems | ការពារកន្លែងលាក់កំបាំង

- **Location is sensitive by nature.** Default to *approximate* pinning;
  use *sensitive* for fragile ecosystems, religious sites in use, private
  homes, or anywhere exposure could cause harm. Never post someone else's
  exact location.
- Leave no trace — don't encourage damage, trespass, or wildlife
  disturbance.

## What is not allowed | អ្វីដែលមិនអនុញ្ញាត

1. Spam & scams (ការឆបោក) — including fake bookings and phishing links.
2. Nudity or sexual content.
3. Violence or incitement.
4. Illegal activity, including wildlife trade.
5. Copyrighted content you don't own (`docs/legal/copyright.md`).
6. Private information of others (addresses, phones, ID documents).
7. Misusing reports, blocks or mutes as harassment tools.
8. Circumventing rate limits, bans, or scraping the service.

## Sponsored content | មាតិកាឧបត្ថម្ភ

Paid placements are always labeled **Sponsored / ដៃគូឧបត្ថម្ភ**, kept
separate from organic results, and never affect rankings. Undisclosed paid
promotion in organic posts is a violation.

## Enforcement | ការអនុវត្ត

Report anything via the ⚑ button. The moderation team (see
`docs/OPERATIONS.md` §5) reviews reports, hides content automatically
when several reports accumulate, and notifies authors of outcomes in-app.
Appeals go to a second moderator. Repeat violations lead to restriction
or removal of the account.
